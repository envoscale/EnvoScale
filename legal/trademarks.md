"EnvoScale", "EnvoL" and the EnvoScale logos (the "**Marks**") identify the official
EnvoScale project and the products of its licensor. The licenses in [`LICENSE`](https://github.com/envoscale/EnvoLang/blob/main/LICENSE)
cover the software, not the Marks. This policy says how you may use the Marks.

## Allowed without asking

- Saying truthfully that your program is written in EnvoL or built with EnvoScale
  ("Written in EnvoL", "Built with EnvoScale").
- Referring to EnvoScale in articles, talks, courses, books, reviews and comparisons.
- Naming a library or tool so that it says it works with EnvoScale, if the name does not
  start with the Marks and does not suggest it is official: `json-for-envo` is fine,
  `EnvoScale JSON` is not.
- Using the unmodified logo next to such a statement or reference.

## Needs written permission

- Using the Marks in the name of a product, company, service, domain or package
  namespace.
- Distributing a **modified** version of EnvoScale under the EnvoScale name. A modified
  build must use a different name and say what it is based on, for example
  "Foo, based on EnvoScale".
- Selling merchandise with the Marks.
- Any use that suggests the licensor endorses or supports you, such as "official",
  "certified" or "partner", unless you have that relationship in writing.

## Never allowed

- Using the Marks in a way that is misleading or confusing about who made something.
- Using names or logos confusingly similar to the Marks.
- Modifying the logos, other than scaling them.

To ask for permission, open an issue in the
[envoscale organization](https://github.com/envoscale) or contact the licensor as
[`LICENSING.md`](LICENSING.md) describes.
